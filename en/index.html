What we make

Books about nature and the life in it

We have only just started, and we say it as it is. We do not have a long back catalogue yet. What we do have is a clear idea of what we want to make: books that get people out into nature, and that give children and adults good experiences together.

For children

Curiosity outdoors

Books that spark the urge to explore, to know the animals and plants, and to feel the seasons.

For adults

Outdoor life and depth

Books about being in nature, slowly and attentively, and about bringing along the people you care for.

Together

Experiences that are shared

We make books you use together, on the trip, in the tent, at the kitchen table afterwards.

The person behind it

Hi, I'm Kasper

Forlaget Uldahl is my idea. I run my own businesses and have built several companies from the ground up, so I know what it takes to bring an idea to life, and how much work sits behind it.

The publisher is new, but the idea behind it is simple: I want to make books about what I care for myself, nature and the good moments in it, and find the right people to make them with.

Partnerships

Shall we make something together?

We are looking for authors, illustrators, photographers and others who want to make books about nature. If you are a potential partner, we would like to hear from you.

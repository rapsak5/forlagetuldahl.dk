For authors

We are looking for authors, illustrators and photographers who want to make books about nature and outdoor life.

Send a short outline or the first pages along with a description of the idea and the audience. We read everything we receive and get back to you once we have been through it.

Forlaget Uldahl is a Danish publishing house. We publish a small number of titles at a time and put the work into each book.

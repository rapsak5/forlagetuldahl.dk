About the publisher

Forlaget Uldahl is a brand new Danish publishing house. We make books about nature and about good experiences out in it, for children and adults alike.

We say it as it is: we have only just started, and we do not have a long list of titles behind us yet. What we do have is a clear idea of what we want to make, and we are finding the right people to make it with.

If you have an idea, a manuscript or would like to work with us, we would like to hear from you.

Forlaget Uldahl is a Danish publishing house. We publish a small number of titles at a time and put the work into each book.
